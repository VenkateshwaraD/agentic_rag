# Digital Banking Services Addendum — BluePeak Capital Partners

<!-- doc_id: AGR-101 | client_id: bluepeak | version: 1.0 | effective_date: 2025-03-01 | doc_type: agreement -->

## §1 Scope
This Digital Banking Services Addendum ("Addendum") supplements the Client
Services Agreement (AGR-002) between Example Bank N.A. ("Bank") and BluePeak
Capital Partners ("Client"). It governs the Bank's digital channels: online
banking, the mobile application, and API connectivity for treasury reporting.

## §2 Remote Deposit Capture
Checks deposited through the mobile application's remote deposit capture
feature are subject to the following limits:

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Per-check maximum | $50,000 |
| Daily aggregate maximum | $150,000 |
| Rolling 30-day maximum | $1,500,000 |

Items exceeding these limits must be deposited at a branch or through a
wholesale lockbox arrangement.

## §3 Electronic Signatures and Records
The Client consents to the use of electronic signatures and electronic
delivery of account records. E-signed instructions are binding to the same
extent as wet-ink signatures under the E-SIGN Act.

## §4 Digital Channel Availability
Target availability for online and mobile banking is 99.5% monthly uptime,
excluding the published maintenance window (Sundays 02:00–04:00 ET).
Availability claims are governed by SLA-001 service credits.
