# Service Level Agreement — Safe Deposit Box Services

<!-- doc_id: SLA-101 | scope: safe_deposit_sla | doc_type: sla | version: 1.0 | effective_date: 2025-01-15 -->

## §1 Access Service Levels
Safe deposit box access is provided during branch lobby hours. A box holder
may request access at any staffed branch; the target wait time from
verification to vault entry is 15 minutes.

| Service | Target |
|---|---|
| Vault entry after ID verification | 15 minutes |
| New box activation after paperwork | 2 business days |
| Lost-key drill-out scheduling | 10 business days |

## §2 Annual Billing
Box rental fees are billed annually on the anniversary of the rental
agreement. Fees vary by box size and are listed in the branch fee schedule;
they are not covered by the client's treasury services agreement.

## §3 Escheat and Dormancy
Boxes with no access activity and unpaid rent for 24 months are processed
under the state's unclaimed-property rules. The Bank sends two written
notices before reporting dormancy.
