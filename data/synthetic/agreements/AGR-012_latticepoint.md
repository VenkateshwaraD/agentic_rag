# Client Services Agreement — LatticePoint Software

<!-- doc_id: AGR-012 | client_id: latticepoint | version: 2.0 | effective_date: 2022-11-01 -->

## §1 Parties and Scope
This Client Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Example Bank N.A.
("Bank") and LatticePoint Software ("Client", client_id `latticepoint`), a commercial
client domiciled in the region served by the Payments Operations team. This Agreement governs
cash-management, payments, and reporting services.

## §2 Fee Schedule
The following fees apply to services rendered under this Agreement:

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Outgoing domestic wire transfer | $20.33 per item |
| ACH origination (per item) | $0.75 |
| Monthly account maintenance | $253.75 |
| Paper statement delivery | $12.34 per statement |

Fees are debited monthly in arrears from the Client's designated operating account.
Electronic statements are provided at no charge.

## §3 Statement Terms
Account statements are produced on a daily basis. Clients on daily
reporting may request ad-hoc intraday statements through the Statements & Reporting
team; ad-hoc requests are subject to the paper statement fee listed in §2 unless
delivered electronically.

## §4 Amendments
This Agreement (version 2.0, effective 2022-11-01) may be amended upon thirty (30)
days written notice. Continued use of services constitutes acceptance of amendments.
Service-related inquiries are handled by the Payments Operations team per §1.
