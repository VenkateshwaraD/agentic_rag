# Client Services Agreement — Kestrel Bay Resorts

<!-- doc_id: AGR-011 | client_id: kestrelbay | version: 2.0 | effective_date: 2023-10-01 -->

## §1 Parties and Scope
This Client Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Example Bank N.A.
("Bank") and Kestrel Bay Resorts ("Client", client_id `kestrelbay`), a small business
client domiciled in the region served by the Escalations Desk team. This Agreement governs
cash-management, payments, and reporting services.

## §2 Fee Schedule
The following fees apply to services rendered under this Agreement:

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Outgoing domestic wire transfer | $15.29 per item |
| ACH origination (per item) | $0.19 |
| Monthly account maintenance | $708.74 |
| Paper statement delivery | $13.47 per statement |

Fees are debited monthly in arrears from the Client's designated operating account.
Electronic statements are provided at no charge.

## §3 Statement Terms
Account statements are produced on a monthly basis. Clients on monthly
reporting may request ad-hoc intraday statements through the Statements & Reporting
team; ad-hoc requests are subject to the paper statement fee listed in §2 unless
delivered electronically.

## §4 Amendments
This Agreement (version 2.0, effective 2023-10-01) may be amended upon thirty (30)
days written notice. Continued use of services constitutes acceptance of amendments.
Service-related inquiries are handled by the Escalations Desk team per §1.
